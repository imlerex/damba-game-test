📖 How to Play Barn Bloom

Welcome to your cozy barn!

  • 🌱 **Planting**: Click an empty pot and choose a seed from the shop to plant it.
  • 💧 **Watering**: Select the Watering Can (Hotkey W) and click pots to keep moisture in the sweet spot (50%-80%). Avoid underwatering (wilting) and overwatering (root rot)!
  • 🌾 **Harvesting**: When plants reach maturity (Hotkey H), harvest them for valuable coins and XP.
  • 🛒 **Shop & Upgrades**: Buy nutrients, expand your barn with more pots, and unlock helper automation bots!
  • ⌨️ **Hotkeys**: W Water, I Inspect, H Harvest, S Clear, B Shop, U Upgrades, ESC Settings.
